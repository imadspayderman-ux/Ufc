HOW TO PLAY

MOVEMENT

  • A / D — walk
  • W — jump / step in
  • S — crouch
  • Shift — dash / dodge

PUNCHES

  • J — jab (fast, low damage)
  • K — cross / hook (heavy)
  • U — uppercut (anti-air, launcher)

KICKS

  • O — low kick (leg, drains stamina)
  • L — mid kick (body)
  • ; — HIGH kick (head, big damage)

DEFENSE & SPECIAL

  • I — block (drains stamina)
  • Shift — dodge (i-frames)
  • Space — SPECIAL (needs full meter)
  • P / Esc — pause

GRAPPLING & GROUND

  • G — clinch (close) or shoot takedown (mid)
  • in clinch: J/K dirty punch, U elbow, L knee
  • in clinch: G takedown, F guillotine, T break
  • on top (mount): J/K ground & pound, U elbow
  • on top: F submission, R advance, T stand up
  • on bottom: F triangle/armbar, T/W stand up
  • being submitted: E mash to tap-escape

Tip: Build SPECIAL meter by landing strikes and taking hits. Specials are unblockable and deal massive damage.

Tip: Low kicks drain the opponent's stamina; head kicks do the most damage but are slow.

Tip: A perfectly-timed dodge (Shift just before a hit) gives a counter window.

Tip: Each fighter has a specialty (Striker, Boxer, BJJ, Wrestler, Muay Thai…). Grapplers shoot takedowns; strikers keep distance. Weight class changes HP, power, and cadence.

Tip: Press G close to clinch. In the clinch, knees and elbows are devastating and can't be blocked. Wrestlers convert clinch → takedown → mount → submission.
